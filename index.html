About

Shawn Ghandchi is a Silicon Valley real estate agent who also develops homes. He represents sellers and buyers in Santa Clara County and San Mateo County, with most of his volume in Menlo Park, Saratoga, Santa Clara, San Jose, Mountain View and Los Altos. Because he has taken properties from lot to finished home, he advises owners of older homes and large lots on whether to sell as-is, renovate, or sell to a builder.
